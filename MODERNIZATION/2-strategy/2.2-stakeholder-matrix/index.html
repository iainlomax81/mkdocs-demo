2.2 Stakeholder Matrix and Feature Prioritization

Document Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-06-09
Status: Modernization Planning

Executive Summary

This document maps modernization features to business value, stakeholder needs, risks, and implementation priorities. It provides a framework for decision-making and stakeholder communication throughout the modernization initiative.

Purpose: Enable stakeholders to: - Understand how modernization features deliver business value - Prioritize features based on stakeholder needs and business impact - Assess risks and dependencies - Track modernization progress against stakeholder expectations

1. Stakeholder Identification and Analysis

1.1 Primary Stakeholders

Stakeholder Group Key Representatives Primary Interests Influence Impact
Executive Leadership CEO, CFO, COO ROI, revenue growth, cost control, risk mitigation 🔴 HIGH 🔴 HIGH
Customers End users (shoppers) Easy shopping, mobile experience, fast checkout, data privacy 🟡 MEDIUM 🔴 HIGH
Marketing Team CMO, Marketing Manager Customer acquisition, conversion rates, international expansion, analytics 🟡 MEDIUM 🟡 MEDIUM
IT Operations IT Manager, DevOps Team System reliability, scalability, security, maintainability 🟡 MEDIUM 🟡 MEDIUM
Development Team Tech Lead, Developers Modern tech stack, automated testing, CI/CD, code quality 🟢 LOW 🟡 MEDIUM
Customer Support Support Manager, Support Reps Self-service capabilities, order tracking, clear error messages 🟢 LOW 🟡 MEDIUM
Legal/Compliance Legal Counsel, DPO (if exists) GDPR compliance, data protection, accessibility, consumer protection 🟡 MEDIUM 🔴 HIGH
Finance CFO, Financial Controller Budget adherence, cost predictability, ROI tracking 🔴 HIGH 🟡 MEDIUM

1.2 Stakeholder Needs and Pain Points

Executive Leadership: - 🎯 Need: Increased revenue and market share - 😓 Pain: Technology obsolescence risk, GDPR non-compliance risk, unable to serve mobile customers - 📊 Success Metric: Revenue growth, cost reduction, risk mitigation

Customers: - 🎯 Need: Easy, fast shopping experience on any device - 😓 Pain: Desktop-only site (poor mobile experience), forced registration before checkout, slow page loads - 📊 Success Metric: Conversion rate, cart abandonment rate, customer satisfaction (NPS)

Marketing Team: - 🎯 Need: Tools to drive customer acquisition and retention - 😓 Pain: Cannot run promotions (no promo code), no customer analytics, no personalization, English-only - 📊 Success Metric: Customer acquisition cost (CAC), conversion rate, average order value (AOV)

IT Operations: - 🎯 Need: Reliable, scalable infrastructure with minimal manual intervention - 😓 Pain: Single-server architecture (single point of failure), cannot scale, manual deployments, no monitoring - 📊 Success Metric: Uptime (99.9%+), deployment frequency, mean time to recovery (MTTR)

Development Team: - 🎯 Need: Modern tools and practices for productive development - 😓 Pain: Obsolete tech stack, no automated tests, difficult to recruit developers, slow feedback loops - 📊 Success Metric: Development velocity, deployment frequency, code quality, developer satisfaction

Customer Support: - 🎯 Need: Self-service tools to reduce support volume - 😓 Pain: Customers cannot track orders, cannot cancel/modify orders, no returns portal - 📊 Success Metric: Support ticket volume, resolution time, customer satisfaction

Legal/Compliance: - 🎯 Need: Regulatory compliance, data protection, risk mitigation - 😓 Pain: GDPR non-compliance, no data subject rights, no cookie consent, accessibility unknown - 📊 Success Metric: Compliance audit results, zero regulatory fines, zero accessibility lawsuits

2. Feature-to-Stakeholder Mapping

2.1 Phase 1 Features (Months 1-3)

Feature 1.1: Cloud Migration (App Service + Managed SQL)

Business Capability: Scalability, resilience, cost optimization

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Executive Leadership Scalability, reduced infrastructure risk, foundation for growth 🔴 HIGH
IT Operations Auto-scaling, reduced maintenance, better reliability 🔴 HIGH
Finance Predictable costs, pay-as-you-go pricing 🟡 MEDIUM
Customers Improved performance, better uptime 🟡 MEDIUM

Business Value: Enables horizontal scaling, eliminates single point of failure, reduces infrastructure management burden.

Risk: Migration downtime (mitigation: blue-green deployment), unexpected cloud costs (mitigation: cost monitoring and budgets).

Dependencies: Cloud provider selection, infrastructure team availability.

Feature 1.2: Mobile-Responsive Design

Business Capability: Mobile commerce, improved UX, SEO

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Customers Excellent mobile shopping experience 🔴 CRITICAL
Executive Leadership Capture mobile revenue (70%+ of traffic) 🔴 CRITICAL
Marketing Team Better SEO, reduced bounce rate, higher conversion 🔴 HIGH
IT Operations Single codebase (no separate mobile app) 🟡 MEDIUM

Business Value: - Revenue Impact: Estimated 30-50% increase in mobile conversion rate - SEO Impact: Google prioritizes mobile-friendly sites (mobile-first indexing) - Brand Impact: Professional, modern brand perception

Risk: Design changes may require customer communication (mitigation: gradual rollout, A/B testing).

Dependencies: UI/UX design resources, responsive CSS framework selection.

Feature 1.3: GDPR Compliance (Privacy Policy, Cookie Consent, Data Deletion)

Business Capability: Regulatory compliance, data protection, customer trust

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Legal/Compliance Avoid fines (up to 4% revenue), regulatory compliance 🔴 CRITICAL
Executive Leadership Risk mitigation, customer trust 🔴 HIGH
Customers Data privacy, control over personal data 🟡 MEDIUM
Marketing Team Build trust for better customer relationships 🟡 MEDIUM

Business Value: - Risk Mitigation: Avoid GDPR fines (€20 million or 4% of global revenue, whichever is higher) - Customer Trust: Privacy-conscious customers more likely to purchase - Competitive Advantage: Demonstrate commitment to data protection

Risk: Legal review required (mitigation: engage data protection consultant early).

Dependencies: Legal counsel review, data protection consultant, privacy policy drafting.

Feature 1.4: Automated Testing Foundation

Business Capability: Quality assurance, faster development, reduced risk

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Development Team Confidence in changes, faster development 🔴 HIGH
Executive Leadership Reduced risk of bugs, faster time to market 🟡 MEDIUM
IT Operations Fewer production issues, easier troubleshooting 🟡 MEDIUM
Customers Fewer bugs, better quality experience 🟡 MEDIUM

Business Value: - Risk Reduction: Catch regressions before production - Development Speed: Faster feature delivery with confidence - Maintenance Cost: Easier to refactor and evolve codebase

Risk: Initial investment in testing slows feature delivery temporarily (mitigation: prioritize critical business rules first).

Dependencies: Testing framework selection, developer training.

2.2 Phase 2 Features (Months 4-6)

Feature 2.1: Guest Checkout

Business Capability: Reduced cart abandonment, improved conversion

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Customers Faster checkout, no forced registration 🔴 CRITICAL
Executive Leadership Reduced cart abandonment, increased revenue 🔴 HIGH
Marketing Team Better conversion rate, lower CAC 🔴 HIGH
Development Team Cleaner checkout flow 🟢 LOW

Business Value: - Cart Abandonment: Estimated 20-25% reduction in cart abandonment - Conversion Rate: Industry data shows forced registration increases abandonment by ~25% - Customer Satisfaction: First-time buyers appreciate frictionless experience

Risk: Anonymous orders may complicate order history (mitigation: offer account creation after order).

Dependencies: Order attribution logic, account linking mechanism.

Feature 2.2: AJAX-Based Interactions (Dynamic Basket, Filters)

Business Capability: Improved UX, faster interactions, modern web experience

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Customers Fast, responsive interactions (no page reload) 🔴 HIGH
Marketing Team Better user engagement, lower bounce rate 🟡 MEDIUM
Development Team Modern development experience 🟡 MEDIUM

Business Value: - User Experience: Faster perceived performance - Engagement: Users more likely to explore and interact - Competitive Parity: Matches modern e-commerce UX expectations

Risk: JavaScript complexity increases (mitigation: use modern framework, comprehensive testing).

Dependencies: JavaScript framework selection (jQuery upgrade or React/Vue), REST API design.

Feature 2.3: Product Search

Business Capability: Product discovery, improved conversion

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Customers Find products quickly without browsing categories 🔴 HIGH
Marketing Team Drive traffic to specific products, better SEO 🟡 MEDIUM
Executive Leadership Improved conversion from search traffic 🟡 MEDIUM

Business Value: - Conversion Rate: Users who search have higher purchase intent - User Experience: Critical feature for e-commerce (industry standard) - SEO: Site search data informs content strategy

Risk: Search relevance requires tuning (mitigation: use proven search library like Elasticsearch or Azure Search).

Dependencies: Search engine selection, product indexing strategy.

Feature 2.4: "Download My Data" (GDPR Data Portability)

Business Capability: GDPR compliance, data subject rights

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Legal/Compliance Full GDPR compliance (Art. 20: data portability) 🔴 HIGH
Customers Control over personal data, trust 🟡 MEDIUM
Executive Leadership Complete compliance, risk mitigation 🟡 MEDIUM

Business Value: - Compliance: Completes GDPR data subject rights implementation - Customer Trust: Transparency about data collection - Competitive Advantage: Demonstrate data protection commitment

Risk: Data export may reveal more data collected than customer expects (mitigation: clear privacy policy).

Dependencies: Data export format (JSON/CSV), customer data aggregation logic.

2.3 Phase 3 Features (Months 7-12)

Feature 3.1: .NET 6+ Migration

Business Capability: Long-term maintainability, modern development, performance

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Development Team Modern development experience, faster development 🔴 HIGH
IT Operations Better performance, official support 🟡 MEDIUM
Executive Leadership Long-term platform viability, attract talent 🟡 MEDIUM

Business Value: - Performance: .NET 6+ is significantly faster than .NET Framework - Support: Long-term support (LTS) from Microsoft - Talent: Easier to recruit developers familiar with modern .NET - Features: Access to latest language features and libraries

Risk: Large migration effort (mitigation: phased migration, extensive testing).

Dependencies: Application architecture review, compatibility analysis.

Feature 3.2: WCAG 2.1 Level AA Compliance

Business Capability: Accessibility, legal compliance, market expansion

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Legal/Compliance Avoid accessibility lawsuits, regulatory compliance 🔴 HIGH
Executive Leadership Risk mitigation, inclusive brand values 🟡 MEDIUM
Customers (with disabilities) Accessible shopping experience 🔴 CRITICAL
Marketing Team Expand addressable market (15% of population has disabilities) 🟡 MEDIUM

Business Value: - Legal Risk Mitigation: Reduce risk of accessibility lawsuits (ADA Title III) - Market Expansion: 15% of global population has disabilities (WHO estimate) - Brand Reputation: Demonstrate commitment to inclusion and accessibility - SEO: Accessible sites often rank better (better semantic HTML)

Risk: Remediation effort may be larger than expected (mitigation: prioritize critical issues first).

Dependencies: Accessibility audit, screen reader testing, remediation expertise.

Feature 3.3: Order Tracking and Management

Business Capability: Customer self-service, reduced support costs

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Customers Track shipment, view order status, cancel/modify orders 🔴 HIGH
Customer Support Reduced support ticket volume (~30-40% reduction expected) 🔴 HIGH
Executive Leadership Lower support costs, better customer satisfaction 🟡 MEDIUM
IT Operations Integration with shipping carriers 🟢 LOW

Business Value: - Support Cost Reduction: Estimated 30-40% reduction in order status inquiries - Customer Satisfaction: Customers want order visibility (industry standard) - Operational Efficiency: Automated order lifecycle updates

Risk: Integration with shipping carriers may be complex (mitigation: use third-party API like EasyPost or Shippo).

Dependencies: Shipping carrier API access, order fulfillment workflow definition.

Feature 3.4: Admin Portal (Product and Order Management)

Business Capability: Operational efficiency, content management

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Marketing Team Add/edit products without developer assistance 🔴 HIGH
IT Operations Manage orders, troubleshoot customer issues 🔴 HIGH
Executive Leadership Operational independence, faster time to market 🟡 MEDIUM
Development Team No longer needed for content updates 🟡 MEDIUM

Business Value: - Operational Efficiency: Non-technical staff can manage content - Time to Market: Add new products without development cycle - Cost Savings: Reduce developer involvement in operational tasks

Risk: Admin security must be robust (mitigation: role-based access control, audit logging).

Dependencies: Admin UI framework, role-based permissions design.

2.4 Phase 4 Features (Months 13+)

Feature 4.1: Personalization Engine

Business Capability: Customer retention, increased AOV

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Marketing Team Personalized recommendations, targeted campaigns 🔴 HIGH
Executive Leadership Increased AOV, customer retention 🔴 HIGH
Customers Relevant product suggestions, time savings 🟡 MEDIUM

Business Value: - Average Order Value (AOV): Estimated 15-20% increase through cross-sell and upsell - Customer Retention: Personalized experience increases repeat purchases - Competitive Advantage: Matches Amazon/Netflix-style recommendations

Risk: Recommendation quality depends on data volume (mitigation: start simple with "frequently bought together").

Dependencies: Customer behavior data collection, recommendation algorithm selection.

Feature 4.2: Progressive Web App (PWA)

Business Capability: App-like experience, offline support, engagement

Stakeholder Mapping:

Stakeholder Value Delivered Priority to Stakeholder
Customers Add to home screen, offline browsing, push notifications 🟡 MEDIUM
Marketing Team Push notifications for abandoned carts, order updates 🟡 MEDIUM
IT Operations Single codebase (no separate mobile app) 🟡 MEDIUM

Business Value: - Engagement: Push notifications increase return visits - User Experience: App-like experience without app store friction - Cost Savings: No separate native mobile app development

Risk: Browser support varies (mitigation: progressive enhancement, fallbacks).

Dependencies: Service worker implementation, push notification service.

4. Risk Assessment Matrix

4.1 Feature Risk Analysis

Feature Technical Risk Business Risk Mitigation Strategy Contingency Plan
Cloud Migration MEDIUM (downtime) HIGH (business disruption) Blue-green deployment, rollback plan Revert to on-premises if critical issues
Mobile Design LOW (CSS framework) LOW (user adaptation) Gradual rollout, A/B testing Maintain desktop fallback
GDPR Compliance LOW (well-defined requirements) HIGH (legal consequences if incomplete) Legal review, data protection consultant Prioritize most critical rights first
Guest Checkout MEDIUM (order attribution) MEDIUM (data quality) Account linking, email verification Require registration for future orders
.NET 6+ Migration HIGH (large codebase migration) MEDIUM (regressions) Phased migration, extensive testing Pause migration if too many issues
WCAG Compliance MEDIUM (scope uncertainty) MEDIUM (legal risk if incomplete) Accessibility audit first, prioritize critical issues Partial compliance with roadmap for full compliance

4.2 Risk Mitigation Framework

Technical Risk Mitigation: - Automated testing (catch regressions early) - Code reviews (ensure quality) - Proof-of-concept (validate approach before full implementation) - Rollback plans (revert if critical issues)

Business Risk Mitigation: - Stakeholder communication (manage expectations) - Phased rollout (limit blast radius) - Monitoring and alerting (detect issues quickly) - Customer communication (announce changes, collect feedback)

Legal/Compliance: - ✅ GDPR compliance audit passed - ✅ Accessibility audit passed (WCAG 2.1 Level AA) - ✅ Zero regulatory violations or fines

6. Communication and Governance

6.1 Communication Plan

Stakeholder Communication Frequency:

Stakeholder Group Communication Type Frequency Content
Executive Leadership Executive Summary Monthly Progress, risks, budget, decisions needed
Project Steering Committee Steering Committee Meeting Bi-weekly Detailed progress, blockers, prioritization
IT Operations Technical Sync Weekly Infrastructure changes, deployments, incidents
Development Team Sprint Planning/Retro Weekly Feature prioritization, technical decisions, blockers
Marketing Team Business Update Monthly Feature releases, customer impact, metrics
Customer Support Training and Updates Per release New features, process changes, training materials
Legal/Compliance Compliance Review Quarterly Compliance status, audit findings, remediation
Customers Product Announcements Per major release New features, improvements, privacy updates

6.2 Decision-Making Framework

Decision Authority:

Decision Type Authority Process
Strategic (e.g., scope changes, budget increases) Executive Sponsor Steering Committee recommendation → Executive approval
Tactical (e.g., feature prioritization within phase) Project Manager + Tech Lead Team consensus → Steering Committee notification
Technical (e.g., technology choices, architecture) Tech Lead + Architects Technical review → Project Manager approval
Operational (e.g., deployment timing, rollback) IT Operations Manager DevOps team recommendation → Ops Manager approval

Escalation Path: 1. Team Lead (day-to-day decisions) 2. Project Manager (tactical decisions, blockers) 3. Steering Committee (prioritization changes, resource allocation) 4. Executive Sponsor (budget, scope, strategic direction)

6.3 Governance Meetings

Steering Committee: - Attendees: Executive Sponsor, Project Manager, Tech Lead, IT Ops Manager, Marketing Manager, Legal Counsel - Frequency: Bi-weekly - Agenda: Progress review, risk assessment, prioritization adjustments, decision-making

Technical Review Board: - Attendees: Tech Lead, Architects, Senior Developers - Frequency: Weekly - Agenda: Architecture decisions, technology choices, technical debt management

Stakeholder Updates: - Attendees: All stakeholders - Frequency: Monthly - Format: Email summary + optional Q&A session - Content: Feature releases, progress highlights, upcoming changes

7. Summary and Recommendations

7.1 Key Stakeholder Priorities

Highest Priority Stakeholder Needs: 1. Customers: Mobile experience (P0 - Critical) 2. Executive Leadership: GDPR compliance and risk mitigation (P0 - Critical) 3. Executive Leadership: Revenue growth through improved conversion (P1 - High) 4. Legal/Compliance: Full regulatory compliance (P0-P2 across phases) 5. IT Operations: Scalability and reliability (P0 - Critical)

7.2 Feature Prioritization Summary

Must Have (Phase 1): - Mobile-responsive design - GDPR compliance (privacy policy, cookie consent, data deletion) - Cloud migration (scalability foundation) - Automated testing (safe modernization)

Should Have (Phase 2): - Guest checkout (reduce cart abandonment) - Product search (industry standard) - AJAX interactions (modern UX) - GDPR data portability (complete compliance)

Could Have (Phase 3): - Order tracking (customer self-service) - WCAG compliance (accessibility) - .NET 6+ migration (long-term maintainability) - Admin portal (operational efficiency)

Won't Have (This Phase, but Phase 4): - Personalization engine - Progressive Web App

7.3 Recommendations

  1. Secure Executive Sponsorship Early: Assign clear executive sponsor with decision-making authority.

  2. Establish Steering Committee: Form steering committee with representatives from all key stakeholder groups.

  3. Communicate Proactively: Use communication plan to keep stakeholders informed and engaged.

  4. Measure Success: Implement Application Insights in Phase 1 to baseline current metrics and track improvements.

  5. Manage Expectations: Be transparent about priorities, trade-offs, and timeline.

  6. Celebrate Wins: Recognize and communicate successes at each phase to maintain momentum.
